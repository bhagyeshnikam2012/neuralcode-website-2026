What is AI?

Artificial intelligence is technology that can perform tasks such as understanding language, recognising patterns, and generating content.

AI systems learn patterns from data or follow programmed rules. They can be useful, but they can also make mistakes.

🗣️

What is a chatbot?

A chatbot is a program that communicates with people through text or voice.

Some chatbots use AI models to understand prompts and generate responses. Others use predefined answers.

💻

What is coding?

Coding means writing instructions that a computer can execute.

HTML structures a webpage, CSS styles it, and JavaScript adds interactive behaviour.
